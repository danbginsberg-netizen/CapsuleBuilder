Built around her store, replenished through authentic designs, meticulously sourced materials and New York fashion

Add what she already carries: a photo of her floor, her store palette, or her item list from a spreadsheet or rep platform. In the Know

Each capsule shows the trends it rides, and the notes are replaced with new insights as the research is refreshed every week.

Seven Retro Forever boards (holiday gift counter, bracelet stacks, warm neutrals, stone strands, sea life, faith and cross, chunky resin) and five for Only If You Know, each built from this season’s trend notes and filled fresh from today’s stock.

Budget built in

Sized to her store and her network

Choose small to very large, or enter her monthly jewelry sales: the opening budget follows her sales and daily store traffic, always at or above the first-order minimum. For chains, one anchor is versioned door by door and delivered as a single workbook.

On an iPad or phone

Guided mode for booths and kiosks

A four-step flow — pick the piece, set the budget or store size, see the capsule, take it away by QR code — for the buyer across the table, an unattended kiosk, or a program page. Prices appear when you tap.

For reps

Training built on our collection

Rep training with coach notes at every step (what she sees, what to say, why) and certification sessions, plus a Her store walk-through that coaches on the buyer’s actual assortment. New reps are ready for a first store call the day after signing.

For approvals

Buyer packets for committees and buying offices

A line sheet built for the capsule plus an item-facts page: materials, suggested retail and quantity, the published terms and the rep’s contact. A committee edition serves museum and nonprofit stores, so a buying office or review committee can approve on its own schedule.

For agencies

Rep kits for MarketTime and RepZio

Ready-to-load item files and every official photo, named by SKU, with upload steps. An agency loads Retro Forever under its own license, and the kit is included with the line.

Sales reps and agencies

Multi-line reps and showrooms deciding whether a line is worth a spot.

Is Retro Forever worth a place in my line-up?

AnswerYes. Retro Forever is a proven design program with a record behind it. The team behind it built Frenzy Jewels into a national jewelry business. Our head designer brings two decades across U.S. retail, from boutiques to national chains, and her work has appeared in New York Fashion Week shows across several seasons. Many of the 240 styles in stock today are repeat best sellers.

What’s differentMost new lines ask a rep to sell a promise. Ours comes with a sell-through record and the supply chain behind it: multiple national chains, 800+ stores across them, reordering 4 seasons annually; directly sourced materials; reliable manufacturing; and production and manufacturing supporting growth while maintaining quality and also enabling higher margins for customers.

Works withThe stores already in your book. Open-price styles at roughly $3–$12 wholesale, a keystone-plus margin and U.S. stock that ships in 1–3 days suit boutiques, gift shops and specialty chains. Commission pays on collected cash: 15%, 18% on quarterly sales above $15,000, and $75 for every account’s first order. Our full proof pack is available on request.

Is the Capsule Builder worth the time it takes to learn?

AnswerYes, for both Retro Forever and Only If You Know. It takes about twenty minutes to learn and builds a store’s buy in about a minute. It already knows each line’s styles, pairings and terms, and its training mode coaches you step by step, with certification, so you are ready for a first store call the day after signing.

What’s differentMost lines hand a rep a catalog and a price list. We hand over a tool that builds a coordinated capsule around the piece the buyer chose, with a reason for every pick, so your time goes to relationships instead of product homework.

Works withIt runs in your browser on a phone, tablet or laptop, next to whatever you already use, with everything included.

How do I get credit for every order from my stores, including reorders?

AnswerEvery capsule link, QR code, line sheet and order form carries your rep code, and our order pages record it on the submitted order. Accounts stay yours on every channel for the life of the agreement.

What’s differentCredit is written onto the order itself, on record for every order, and every capsule has its own ID so a reorder traces back to the capsule that opened the account.

Works withIf you book in MarketTime, the capsule imports straight into your basket; the order stays yours either way.

I already write orders in MarketTime, RepZio or NuORDER. Do I have to switch?

AnswerYou keep your system. The Capsule Builder decides the buy; you book it wherever you already book.

What’s differentOrder-writing systems record what a buyer has already chosen. The coordinated capsule built from one piece the buyer loved, drawn from the full catalog, is our step.

Works withMarketTime Quick Import file, NuORDER order import file, rep kits that load the line into MarketTime and RepZio, SKU line sheets for JOOR, a SKU list for Faire, or our own pre-filled order page.

How do I follow up quickly after a market?

AnswerOne button sends it: your own email opens with the buyer’s capsule link and a pre-filled order link already written in. Review it and press send. At the booth, the buyer scans a QR code and walks away with the capsule on her phone.

What’s differentThe follow-up is the store’s own buy, with photos and reasons, in place of a generic catalog attachment.

Works withYou send it from your own email account, in your own words.

How does the Capsule Builder strengthen my presentation to buyers?

AnswerEvery capsule arrives merchandised and display-ready: necklaces for height, bracelets to stack, earrings as the easy add-on, and a plain-English “Why these pieces” note covering the color story, colorways and matching pieces, plus market notes on the current trends it rides.

What’s differentYou present a merchandised plan built around the buyer’s own favorite piece and backed by market research, so she has a plan ready instead of a catalog to sort.

Works withThe capsule prints as a one-page line sheet and saves as a PDF for her files, with an optional market brief page she can share with her team.

Works withYou can still adjust quantities, swap styles or add your own picks before you order.

My budget is small. Can I still get a real assortment?

AnswerYes. The minimum is $100, and the builder works within your budget: it fills each category first and tells you exactly what fits. Sold by the dozen, and first orders may include a limited number of ½-dozen styles so a small order still covers the display.

What’s differentMany lines set minimums at $250 to $500 and leave you to work out the mix. Ours starts where your budget is.

How do I reorder what sells?

AnswerYour capsule is saved, so the reorder starts from what you bought. Reorders are by the dozen from our U.S. warehouse and ship in 1–3 days.

What’s differentIn stock means a style that sells out returns to your counter from U.S. stock in days.

Works withReorder through your rep, our order page, or Faire if that is where you like to check out.

Can I see it all before I commit?

AnswerYes. Your capsule link opens on any phone: every piece, why it was chosen, the terms and a printable line sheet. The order button opens our order page with the capsule filled in, and you review it and submit before anything is ordered.

What’s differentYour capsule opens in any browser and comes included with the line.

Works withForward the link to a partner or a buyer on your team; it works the same for them.

How is this different from browsing Faire or a line sheet?

AnswerA line sheet lists every style; a marketplace lists every brand. The Capsule Builder starts with your store and what the market is launching this season: a floor photo, your palette or the pieces you already carry, so the buy complements what is on your shelves. It builds one coordinated capsule from a piece you chose, or from one of twelve trend-built starter boards, sizes it to your sales and traffic, and explains every pick.

What’s differentIt is backed by market intelligence. In the Know, our own market research, tracks 37 fashion-jewelry brands and more than 34,600 styles for Retro Forever alone, refreshed every week, and each capsule shows the current trends it rides. It is a service the brand performs for you, included with the line, before you place an order.

Works withThe capsule prints as a one-page line sheet, with an optional market brief page. If you prefer Faire’s checkout, we can put the same Retro Forever capsule there for you.
